Getting these figures into the P&L

Download for P&L gives one line per FD per day with van maintenance costs the business paid (services, repairs, breakdowns, tyres, MOTs). Costs paid by the lease company, warranty or insurance are left out. Damage costs aren't included here: they reach the P&L from the Damages & Claims log, so nothing is counted twice.

Download van hire & insurance gives each FD's lease cost and insurance per 4 weeks (monthly lease × 12 ÷ 13, annual premium ÷ 13), for the P&L's Wages, van & insurance tab.

Each van's costs go to the FD set on the van. Set it under Vans → edit.
